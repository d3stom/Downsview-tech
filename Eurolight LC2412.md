<details>
<summary>Anatomy of the board</summary>
<b> The left hand side</b> <br>
First row of sliders are called presets.(A) The numbers show 13-24 but they are refereed to as preset 1-12.<br>
The second row of sliders are called memory(B). 1-12 <br>
The buttons at the bottom when pressed turn on memory 1-12 <br><br>
<b>The right hand side</b><br>
Top row of buttons for programming (see details below for specific uses)<br>
LCD display - It's a display<br>
SOFT A and SOFT B do what is written on the LCD directly above them<br>
Slider A B and MAIN. <br>
Slider A is a dimmer for all of the presets<br>
Slider B is a dimmer for all of the Memories<br>
Slider MAIN is the dimmer for both
</details>

<details><summary>The current layout</summary>
Circuits 11 and 12 are still using the analogue system. These are on the bar in the middle of the hall and are used for: <br>
* flood lighting<br>
* lighting the thrust stage<br>
* spot lights<br><br>
Circuits 1-10 are for DMX and run on the stage<br>
we have several different DMX lights.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Normal workflow</summary>
<details><summary>Clear all memory</Summary>
Hold SOFT A and SOFT B <br>
Press FLASH 1 <br>
turn the DATA wheel until showing DELETE ALL<br>
use SOFT buttons to confirm delete</details>
<details><summary>Clear DMX patches</Summary>
Press UTILITY 2 (top row) <br>
Press SHIFT (under LCD)<br>
</details>
<details><summary>DMX 1:1</Summary></details>
<details><summary>DMX patching</Summary>
Press UTILITY 2<br>
Press SOFT A - Enter into patch mode<br>
Rotate DATA dial to desired DMX address<br>
Press Desired FLASH number (1-12) <br>
Press SOFT B to connect<br>
</details>
<details><summary> DMX unpatch</summary></details>
<details><summary>Creating memories</Summary>
Press MEMORY to enter memory mode <br>
Press LIVE to see how the lights will look when you add them to memory<br>
Move sliders to where you want them<br>
Press the flash button associated (1 - 12) with the memory number you want (at the bottom of the board)<br>
Press SOFT A for presets or SOFT B for preset and memory to be applied to that memory<br>
Press Quit to leave memory mode
</details>
